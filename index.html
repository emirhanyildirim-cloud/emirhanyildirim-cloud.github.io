RESEARCH SOFTWARE / MVPS

Prototype the boundary,

Applied systems are treated as test beds for invariance, provenance, bounded adaptation, representation and recoverability.

Research MVP

Humanization AI / Bounded Policy-Evolution MVP

Persistent cognitive-systems architecture with typed events, workspace, memory, self/world records, metacognitive signals, restricted executable policy generation, isolated evaluation, audit artefacts and rollback-compatible boundaries.

Claims remain focused on error-aware review routing rather than consciousness or unrestricted self-modification.
Research backend

Prometheus Core & Research Backend

Auditable representation and file/data infrastructure supporting provenance, integrity, reversible processing, manifests and multimodal human/biological workflows.

Internal technical testing reconstructed 47/47 files with SHA-256 verification and no missing, extra or mismatched files.
Imaging MVP

Prometheus MRI MVP

Integrity-gated MRI capsule research prototype with Q8, Q16 and exact fallback, downstream task preservation, anomaly-ranking analysis and cryptographic provenance.

Internal study reported ~4.002× float-array compression with minimal segmentation-behavior drift and a separately gated ~8.165× anomaly profile; no clinical-equivalence claim.
Systems MVP

Prometheus RouterKey / MoE Adapter MVP

Training-free event codec for compact, integrity-protected representation of mixture-of-experts router states.

Reported 0.86% event density, 97.77% top-1 expert agreement and 99.16% top-8 overlap; full end-to-end acceleration remains unproven.
Rehabilitation research MVP

RhythmWalk / Autonomous RAS Research MVP

Fixed-first personalised rhythmic auditory guidance informed by participant-held-out action ablation, cadence calibration, fail-closed quality checks, persistent audit logging and an optional bounded one-window slowdown probe.

No treatment-benefit or adaptive-superiority claim.
Systems R&D

Hardware-Aware Offline GLM System

Active R&D for a locally operated GLM-family model environment intended to select execution, expert residency and fallback strategies according to RAM, storage, CPU/GPU resources and model metadata.

Current validation is limited to router-state representation rather than full-runtime equivalence.
