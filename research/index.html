01 / RARI

Falsification as operating method.

The current RARI programme is structured around perturbation, observer/filter reconstruction, agency and recoverability. Synthetic phases have been used as controlled test beds; prospective biological validation remains a separate step rather than being implied by model behaviour.

Current position

Repeated synthetic perturbation expanded a local identifiable-rank measure from 8 to 17 in completed phases, while the observer-specific inverse/recovery bridge remained unresolved.

Synthetic evidence is not presented as biological validation.

03 / BOUNDARIES

Status is part of the architecture.

Research ≠ implementation

Foundational research programmes are kept separate from implementation ventures and product systems.

Preprints, technical disclosures, prototypes and in-progress experiments are not represented as granted patents, completed companies or independently validated scientific laws.
