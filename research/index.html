03

RESEARCH LOGIC

RARI

Observer, agency, identity & falsification

Studies how source or world states are transformed by observer-specific filters, which structures remain identifiable under perturbation, and which claims about identity or agency survive falsification.

OCIL / OCIR

Language, representation & reference semantics

Develops programme-level language and intermediate-representation work for observer-conditioned information, with transformation, uncertainty, provenance, equivalence classes and recoverability explicit.

AFC

Annihilation-Free Computing

Tests whether computation can be constructed from relational distinctions, transformations and preserved invariants without treating zero as a primitive computational state.

SACRA

Symbolic authority, conversion & ritual agency

Investigates how symbolic authority, ritual practice, repentance or conversion, tradition and perceived external agency can reorganize identity and state transitions.
